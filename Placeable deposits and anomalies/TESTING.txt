PLACEABLE DEPOSITS AND ANOMALIES - V2 TEST

1. Replace your existing "Placeable deposits and anomalies" mod folder with this one.
2. Start/restart Surviving Mars: Relaunched.
3. Enable the mod in Mod Manager.
4. Load a colony.
5. Open Build Menu > Storages.
6. Look for "Map Placement" near the end of the Storages category.

If you already have the Mod Editor open:
- Reload the mod, or close/reopen the editor.
- This V2 also registers on ModsReloaded, so a full game restart should normally
  not be required, but a restart is the cleanest test.
